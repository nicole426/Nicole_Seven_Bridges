A Note on Seven Bridges Sales Activity

If you look at the MLS, you'll see far fewer closed sales in Seven Bridges than what's actually happening on the ground. That number is misleading.

The real number is 4 transactions so far in 2026.

The difference comes from private developer transactions, direct family transfers, and non-MLS deals that never appear in public records. These are deals that happen inside the community — between people who already know each other, or through builders working directly with buyers.

This is exactly why community-level knowledge matters. The MLS tells part of the story. I track all of it.
